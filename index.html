Camera scoring BETA

Point your phone's camera at the board and the app reads each dart for you in X01. You can correct any dart with one tap.

Best results: phone on a stand about 1 metre away, a little below the board, with steady, even light. A ring light helps a lot. If you move the phone, line the board up again.
