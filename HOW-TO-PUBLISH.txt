DARTS NEWS SCORING APP - putting it online as your own app

1. Create a free account at github.com.
2. Tap + (top right) > New repository. Name it: darts-news. Choose Public. Tap Create repository.
3. On the new repository page tap "uploading an existing file".
4. Upload ALL the files from this folder (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js and the four .png icons). Tap Commit changes.
5. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" pick main and / (root), then Save.
6. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your app is at:  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/darts-news/
7. Open that link on your phone (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android) and Add to Home Screen.

To update the app later, upload the new index.html to the same repository (it replaces the old one).
